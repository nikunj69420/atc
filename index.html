On the frequency

Left click and drag the radar to move the map. Scroll over the radar to zoom around the cursor. Use + / − to zoom or the percentage button to reset the view. The Traffic button opens the optional traffic panel.

Select an aircraft with a left click. Click waypoints or any radar position to add vectors. Double click the final point to activate the route. Escape cancels plotting.

Right click an aircraft to change altitude or speed. Use the mouse wheel over either control, or select an exact value. Unrestricted resumes the aircraft’s default climb, descent or speed.

Arrivals land westbound on runway 27. Route an arrival east of the airport, then across the dashed localiser at an intercept angle below 60°, at or below 4,000 ft. Clear ILS or localiser before intercepting.

ILS capture manages descent and speed. Localiser capture manages heading; issue ILS to enable automatic descent. Once ILS established, transfer to tower. Departures leave your sector at the boundary.

Aircraft labels show current → assigned altitude (hundreds of feet), speed in knots, heading and clearance. UNR means unrestricted; GS means automatic glide-path descent. Final sequence shows distance to runway, estimated touchdown time (MM:SS) and gap to the aircraft ahead; red gaps are below 3 NM. Localiser-only estimates are marked ~.

Storm cells drift across the sector. Stay outside their dashed 3 NM buffer. Red weather cores and amber proximity alerts require vectors around the weather. Weather is simulated, not live; storm avoidance distances are simplified for gameplay.

Representative airline ICAO callsigns and aircraft performance. Airspace, fixes and procedures are simplified for gameplay.
